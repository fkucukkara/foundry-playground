# HR Policy: Expense Reimbursement

- Submit expense reports within **30 days** of the purchase date.
- Reimbursable categories: travel, client meals (up to $75/person), home-office equipment (up to $300/year), professional certifications.
- Reimbursements are paid on the next payroll cycle after approval.
- To ask about an expense, open an **HR** ticket with category `Expense Reimbursement` and attach receipts.
