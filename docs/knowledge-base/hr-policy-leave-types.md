# HR Policy: Leave Types & Balances

- Full-time employees accrue **18 vacation days** and **10 sick days** per calendar year.
- Vacation days accrue monthly (1.5 days/month) and can be carried over up to **5 days** into the next year.
- Sick days do not carry over and reset every January 1st.
- To check your current balance, ask the assistant for your leave balance, or open an **HR** ticket with category `Leave Balance`.
- To request time off, submit a request at least **2 weeks in advance** for planned leave.
