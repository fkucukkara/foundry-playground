# IT Policy: Password Reset

- Employees can reset their own Windows/M365 password via the self-service portal at any time.
- If the self-service portal is unavailable, open an **IT** ticket with category `Password Reset` and your employee ID.
- Standard password resets are resolved within **4 business hours**.
- Passwords must be at least 12 characters, include one number and one symbol, and cannot reuse the last 5 passwords.
- After 3 failed reset attempts, the account is locked for 30 minutes for security.
