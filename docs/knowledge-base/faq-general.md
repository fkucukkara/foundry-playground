# General FAQ

**Q: How do I contact IT or HR directly?**
A: Use this chat assistant — it can answer policy questions and open tickets on your behalf for both IT and HR categories.

**Q: How do I check the status of a ticket I already opened?**
A: Ask the assistant "what's the status of ticket #<id>" and it will look it up for you.

**Q: What information do I need to provide to open a ticket?**
A: Your employee/user ID, the category (IT or HR), a short subject, and a description of the issue.
