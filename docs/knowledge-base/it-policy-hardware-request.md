# IT Policy: Hardware Requests

- Standard laptop refresh cycle is every **3 years**.
- To request new hardware (laptop, monitor, peripherals), open an **IT** ticket with category `Hardware Request` describing the item and business justification.
- Standard requests are fulfilled within **5 business days**; urgent requests (broken/lost device) are fulfilled within **1 business day**.
- Loaner laptops are available from the IT desk while a replacement is being processed.
